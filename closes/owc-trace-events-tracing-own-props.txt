owc-trace-events-tracing-own-props resolved witness-70a6ece51d9c21d0 2026-11-02T05:00:00Z

Symptom
  A `trace_events` tracing object owned `categories` and `enabled` as writable data
  properties, so `Object.keys(createTracing({ categories: ['node.async_hooks'] }))`
  was `["categories","enabled"]` in the container and `[]` on host node v24.15.0,
  where both are accessors of the prototype: the object spread, serialised and
  enumerated differently from a host's -- `{"categories":"node.async_hooks","enabled":false}`
  against `{}`, and a spread that copied both against one that copied neither.
  `enable` and `disable` were enumerable on the prototype here and are not on node.

Fix
  packages/core/src/process/executors/node/runtime/guest/modules/pure-b.ts: what
  node's `Tracing` keeps in a private field this one now keeps beside the object
  rather than on it, in a `WeakMap` the factory closes over, so an instance carries
  no own property at all. `categories` and `enabled` are getter-only accessors of
  the prototype, as node's are, and `enable` and `disable` are non-enumerable
  methods there, as node's are; the prototype's own names are the host's five in
  the host's order -- `constructor`, `enable`, `disable`, `enabled`, `categories`.
  The two getters answer what they answered before, and `enable()` and `disable()`
  still answer `undefined` and move the flag.
